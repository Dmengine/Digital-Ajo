How a round works

1

Set up the group

Choose the amount, how often, and who collects in which round. Invite members with a link.

2

Members pay the collector

Each round, everyone sends money by bank transfer to whoever's turn it is, then taps "I've paid".

3

The admin confirms

When the collector says it arrived, the admin ticks it off. The next round starts when everyone is confirmed.

We keep the record. You keep the money.

ContriPay is a planning and reminder tool. We don't take deposits, pay interest or move money for you. Every naira goes from one member's bank account to another's.

Rules baked into the screens

No wallet, no balance

Money only appears as the round amount and who it goes to.

Admin confirms

Only the admin marks a payment as paid. "I've paid" from a member just tells the admin.

No returns language

No growth, interest or earnings anywhere. Savings show as rounds and turns.

Honest checks

"Confirmed" for a bank account only after a real name check. BVN or NIN shows "Submitted".
